# Tasks

This is **Phase 1.5** of the coordinated server redesign, executed immediately after `introduce-client-sessions` (Phase 1) and before `redesign-playlist-and-playback-queue` (Phases 2 and 3). Follow [design.md](./design.md) and [agent-first-project-documentation delta](./specs/agent-first-project-documentation/spec.md). All Java paths are relative to `src/main/java/com/gravifon/player/`; tests mirror them under `src/test/java/com/gravifon/player/`.

Remove `extends AggregateRoot<Track, TrackId>` and `getId()` from `registry/model/Track.java` (retaining `id()` and `trackId()`) and remove the `track.getId()` assertion in `src/test/java/com/gravifon/player/registry/model/TrackPolymorphismTest.java`. Remove `implements Identifier` from `registry/model/TrackId.java`, `session/model/UserId.java`, and `session/model/ClientSessionId.java`. Remove `@DomainRing`, `@ApplicationRing`, and `@InfrastructureRing` imports/annotations from `error/EntityNotFoundException.java`, `registry/metadata/*`, `registry/repository/*`, `registry/scan/*`, `registry/stream/*`, `session/repository/*`, and `session/service/*`.
- [ ] 1.2 Rewrite `src/test/java/com/gravifon/player/architecture/DomainArchitectureArchUnitTest.java` using plain ArchUnit (`@AnalyzeClasses(packages = "com.gravifon.player", importOptions = ImportOption.DoNotIncludeTests.class)` with `.allowEmptyShould(true)` so rules also hold before `playlist`/`playback` packages exist) to enforce:

    - `..session..` must not depend on `..registry..`, `..playlist..`, `..playback..`, `..streaming..`, `..api..`, or `..observability..`.
    - `..registry..` must not depend on `..session..`, `..playlist..`, `..playback..`, `..streaming..`, `..api..`, or `..observability..`.
    - `..playlist..` must not depend on `..playback..`, `..streaming..`, `..api..`, `..observability..`, `..session.service..`, `..registry.service..`, `..registry.scan..`, `..registry.metadata..`, or `..registry.stream..`.
    - `..playback..` must not depend on `..playlist.service..`, `..playlist.repository..`, `..streaming..`, `..api..`, or `..observability..`.
    - `..streaming..` must not depend on `..playlist..`, `..persistence..`, `..api..`, or `..observability..`.
    - `..persistence..` must not depend on `..session..`, `..registry..`, `..playlist..`, `..playback..`, `..streaming..`, `..api..`, `..config..`, or `..observability..`.

    - `..model..` and `..identity..` (excluding `..api.model..`) must not depend on `org.springframework..`, `jakarta..`, `org.jaudiotagger..`, `lombok..`, or `..service..`, `..repository..`, `..persistence..`, `..api..`, `..config..`, `..observability..`, `..streaming..`, `..scan..`, `..metadata..`, `..stream..`.

    - Public methods of classes in `..repository..` must not have raw return types or raw parameter types in `com.gravifon.player.persistence..` or `org.springframework.data..`.
  Verify `mvn test -Dtest=DomainArchitectureArchUnitTest,TrackPolymorphismTest,IdentifierTest` passes.
